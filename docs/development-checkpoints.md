# Development checkpoint history

These notes preserve earlier checkpoint details and links. Each test result applies to the ROM hash it names. For current availability and build instructions, see the [project README](../README.md).

The earlier October 30 playable-roster update (`18de0155`) unlocks **Super Maaa, Rogue Dawn, Goku, Gimmick and Donkey Kong**. That checkpoint had 52 playable and 28 coming-soon identities. Kamikaze Mario now uses sprites from **Kamikaze V1**, with Mario's native drawing, physics, abilities and palette. Donkey Kong has a larger full-body preview; Burns, Smith, Iron and Beans use their original Contra Force gameplay sprites and colors. Rogue Dawn's new-game health, recursive area reset, and missing menu/lives portraits are fixed. Checks include every menu identity, Rogue's cold-start movement, a Kamikaze 1-1 clear, and 288 comparisons with native Mario. Performance and broader roster playthroughs remain in progress. See the [exact builds, checks and limits](qa/expanded-playable-roster-2026-10-30.json); the frozen source is retained under `docs/qa/checkpoints/2026-10-30/expanded-playable-roster/`.
