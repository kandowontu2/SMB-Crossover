## Shared route and capture runner

`tools/playthroughs/roster_run.py` copies the ROM, labels, debug symbols, policy
and harness into each output directory. Discovery exports every applied input.
`--replay` consumes the complete exported stream with strict controller-poll and
frame synchronization. Only a successful complete replay is marked verified.

`--record` records native emulator video/audio, then encodes an MP4 and removes
the intermediate AVI after successful encoding. The runner checks workspace
size before recording and reserves the bounded raw capture plus512MiB under
the30GB cap. It
retains only stage-entry and endpoint PNGs rather than per-frame screenshots.

Policy interface: return `function(context)` yielding ordinary button names.
The harness owns title/menu navigation and normally calls the policy only during
active gameplay (mode 1, task 3, engine 8). An explicit `policyAllModes` setting
also permits ordinary postmenu controller inputs during injury/automatic modes
for logical-tick retiming. Fixed-input replay is unchanged. Context includes read-only named-RAM access,
live terrain cells, nearest-enemy telemetry, position/velocity, stage and prior
buttons. `--prefix` can preserve a validated controller prefix while discovering
its suffix. It does not mark that discovery as a completed proof.

## Confirmed Scrooge defects

1. **Cane terrain hits used the wrong world page.** A `CPX` between the low-byte
   and high-byte additions overwrote carry, making ordinary right-facing hits
   use `Player_PageLoc+1`. `tests/scrooge-ability-arena.lua` reproduces the miss
   in the unmodified v5 checkpoint: a probe at x120/y200 uses page1 while its
   player stands on page0, leaving the brick intact. The source now performs
   uninterrupted 16-bit addition/subtraction independently for each direction.
   The expanded arena also checks all 66 direction/position combinations at
   x0..8 and x232..255, question rewards, native debris/SFX, protected terrain,
   and pogo engagement/rebound/release.
2. **Later cane attacks could not damage an already-hit enemy slot.** Scrooge
   shares the Samus type10 damage path, which records per-attack enemy bits in
   `SmBombHits[SmSlot]`. Those bits were never reset for his next swing or pogo.
   `tests/scrooge-cane-enemies.lua` confirms that v5's first swing applies exactly
   100 damage but its second swing fails to damage the same native enemy. The
   source resets slot0's mask only when a new cane strike or pogo begins or a
   pogo rebounds, and explicitly selects slot0 before the shared damage call.
3. **Pogo fell through durable enemies after damaging them.** Its old rebound
   check counted defeated enemies, so a successful nonlethal hit supplied no
   lift. `tests/scrooge-pogo-enemies.lua` reproduces that failure at the first
   hit against a durable native enemy in v5. The source now detects newly
   contacted slots, retains the shared type10 immunity rules, and rebounds on
   each successful hit. The next descent can apply another independent hit.
   Scrooge's dispatcher also now advances the existing brief enemy flash timer,
   which the expanded-character early return previously bypassed.
