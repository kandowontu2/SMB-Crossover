ONE small product proposal, not an implemented optimization
Narrow only Bill's early RemainingWeapons cache admission: preserve the
existing fresh-Select path, and otherwise call GuestPrepareEnemies only
if the real ten-slot pool contains a type32..46 consumer. Ordinary1..8
shots are skipped by RemainingWeapons::Shots, while the later RosterWeapons
batch refreshes its own cache before ordinary collision. Keep all freeze
counters, source input, movement, bullet birth, collisions and later refresh.
BillAdvancedBirth has its own fresh preparation before immediate collision.
Do not skip the whole RemainingWeapons function or all cache refreshes.

Both observed preparations occur40 times. Exact nested arithmetic isolates
10,121 core cycles in the early preparation and10,121 in the later one:
RemainingWeapons inclusive-exclusive=10,121; RosterWeapons residual after
BulletBlocks/BulletEnemies=10,121; cache total=20,242. Avoiding the early
core would remove253.025 cycles/update in this workload, plus its farcall
routing cost, MINUS the new classification scan. Net saving is unmeasured;
this small change alone cannot be called a lag fix.

Required before any candidate acceptance: actual assembled branch/space/
