Actual CPU work
The original duration calculation repeatedly adds a four-bit tempo value.
The experiment replaces its repeated intermediate additions with a256-byte
multiplication table and the original final ADC/DEC. That final ADC retains
the original carry/overflow result. The real saved-channel X is restored;
Y and the full final source-private state remain exact. No ticks, songs,
channels, requests or original ordered APU stores are removed.

The27-byte helper and256-byte table occupy checked FF writer-reserve bytes
at historical A123:A200/A300. No RAM is allocated. This is intentionally a
binary experiment: its retained historical link outputs do not invent names
for the new helper. Those addresses are not current-source reservations.

Remaining integration and acceptance
The historical adapter cannot be copied into current production. Its old
A123 conflicts with accepted Mac music; current source reserves A63/B124 and
has real CV3/cold consumers in E125. Current CV3 owns DMC owner3, so a future
TMNT rebase must use owner4 with complete owner, tail and preemption checks.
Any connected-stream relocation must prove full pointer closure and original
golden parity. A canonical source build, current mirror/IRQ/ordinary-roster
qualification and real same-work attack cadence still precede adoption.
