--------------

indexed base5B70 is ARITHMETIC ONLY: +nativeX accesses5C40..5C6F exactly.

module does not read joypads, advance world clocks, or apply pause policy.

7F alternates real frames4E/4F; explosion82 uses3C/4A/49/4A/4D/4A/4D/F7.
 Native frameF7 emits NO sprite parts. Final action0 still has one final draw
 boundary, as the source does. Call ClearProjectileColor after real rendering.

action0, depending birth parity; explosion begins at tick23 or24 and removal
