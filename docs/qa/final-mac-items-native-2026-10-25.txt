What this fixture improves
--------------------------
The older physical-pickup test explicitly set PlayerStatus, HP, stars and injury
state before portions of its tests. The new fixture gets every resource/result
through actual game code. It prohibits fixture writes to PlayerStatus, HP, stars,
stamina, attack/super, hurt/invulnerability, ready/life/rest/input-edge state,
InjuryTimer, star/master/game timers, lives, death flags, selection, pickup type,
inventory/stamp and cheat state. It does not inject an item actor or move a
post-rise item into the player. Resource-write observers also reject fixture
boundary alias writes to HP/stars/stamina/status.

Disclosed component arrangement still writes ordinary world/Goomba/motion state
at native GameLoop boundaries. It creates flat dry terrain and a question block
with its matching nametable four tiles and palette attributes. Thus the logical
collision cell and visible block occupy the same place. Ordinary Goombas use
their real ID6, standing Y184 and source small bounding-box control9. The native
engine handles contact, item reveal/rise/collection, attacks, injury and death.

Six separate contacts establish the wounded state. After each contact the actor
is removed, both InjuryTimer AND LmInvul/LmHurt expire naturally, Mac lands and
the actor remains absent for at least four updates before the next setup. No
protection timer or injury state is reset to hurry that sequence.

Actual menus choose Little Mac. Both scripts use explicit numeric mode0/1 and
assert actual CrossoverBehaviorVersion readback before collecting data. Verified
Lua54 compilation parsed both bound scripts before a native worker was launched.
