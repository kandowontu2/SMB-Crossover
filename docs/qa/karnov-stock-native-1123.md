# Karnov's hidden 1-UP and Star in Easy 1-1

Mesen launched Downloads `ffd70810`. The replays highlighted Rygar and
pressed Select for Karnov before starting Easy 1-1. They used normal
controller input and saved no captures. The linked tests cover the
[Mushroom, Fire Flower, Boots, Bomb and Ladder](karnov-five-current-native-1113.md).

- **Hidden 1-UP.** Karnov hit the hidden block at X1024 from the floor and
  intercepted the Mushroom at player X1074/Y176. The extra life and its
  cue followed the pickup. He completed 25 more grounded movement
  updates with three lives, his shot and an empty equipment inventory.
- **Star.** Karnov collected the Mushroom from X336 and earned his shot.
  He hit the Star block at X1616 and collected the Star at player
  X1667/Y171. The Star activated, played its music and awarded 1,000
  points. Karnov landed at X1668/Y176 with invincibility still active,
  his shot and both lives after more than 60 movement updates.

The routes used the existing 1-1 pipe platforms at left X688 and X872,
centers X704 and X888, initial top Y208. Neither route summoned a platform.
