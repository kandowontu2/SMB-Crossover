Parodius crowded-body visibility: read-only study, 2026-10-25

The visibility issue remains open. No production graphics, palette, renderer,
sprite limit or actor behavior changed in this study. A private native trial
is assigned separately. Do not describe these models as a shipped fix.

Exact current art
The unchanged-art repacking probe covers all131 generated body poses across
Octopus, TwinBee and Pentarou. The installed CHR equals the packed manifest.
Reusing the existing palette-plane optimizer reduces neither part count nor
peak sprites per scanline in any pose. Repeating the same packing is not a fix.

Body part order is a separate lead
Every generated body pose has disjoint opaque palette planes. Consequently,
reordering those parts can preserve every unlimited-composition pixel, palette,
CHR byte, tile/part count, pose length and renderer cost. The renderer emits
the source records in reverse order, ordinarily admitting small white/black
planes before large colored core parts when the eight-sprite limit is reached.

The read-only model tests two deterministic actual-OAM orders: primary palette
first, then descending opaque pixel count; or descending opaque count alone.
All131 unlimited body compositions remain exact. In isolated eight-sprite
models, either policy improves nine large Octopus poses and regresses none
by total admitted pixel count. This says nothing about native scheduling.

Six actual earlier8ff DMA-buffer snapshots at row IDs583,584,585,587,588,589
match the complete seven-part Octopus body0:71, including wrapped OAM slots.
These row IDs are NOT asserted visible PPU frame IDs. The actual8ff images
586/587 were previously reviewed as sparse body visibility; the source/image
epoch must still be bound by a fresh observer before attributing exact pixels.

In those snapshots, primary-first modeling changes admitted body pixels:
226->226,159->184,21->61,200->218,134->202,222->222.
For row585, primary-palette pixels12->56; other visible actor pixels stay90.
All six models retain the same other-actor pixels and unlimited physical
composition. This is a useful lead, not proof of fairness for every actor,
source alias, rotation, palette, pose, screen edge or actual NES evaluation.

The next private trial must preserve nonbody/weapon records and generator
reproducibility, check record aliases and unchanged public addresses/RAM/CHR,
then bind actual source body publication, DMA, startFrame hardware OAM/CHR and
completed PPU counters. Crowded weapon/enemy visibility, body readability and
completed-update/draw cadence require native qualification. No colors may be
discarded and no actors hidden to make the result appear better.
