Bounded CPU-only cost follow-up
-------------------------------
cpu-cost-profile.json executes one real initialization and600 source play
updates through ExpandedAudioUpdate, including its graphics-bank publication.
It excludes the shared mixer, interrupts, PPU and real DMC DMA stalls. The
baseline uses its existing Rygar fallback; the candidate uses native TMNT song2.
Mean component work actually falls1776.52 to1710.68 cycles, but individual
native updates add as much as4400 cycles. Candidate peak is6031 cycles at
profile tick505, with16 source-writer calls. The corresponding original NSF
source call already costs5183 cycles; most of that burst is original engine
work rather than the new wrapper. This is component attribution, not exact
time alignment to a particular skipped draw in the native performance arena.

One small unimplemented lead is removing PHP/PLP in specialized writer call
sites whose immediately following load makes N/Z dead. Only433 such calls
occur across this whole bounded initialization/play sequence, so its7-cycle
per-call saving alone is small. The generic writer ABI must retain its flags;
a specialized change would require call-site and all-song equivalence proofs.
No source-equivalent small optimization has been established by this follow-up,
and the candidate remains held. No tick rate, tempo or voice was changed.
