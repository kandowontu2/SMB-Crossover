Source behavior to port without Mario substitutes
------------------------------------------------
HeldF7/freshF5: A80 jump,B40 charge/release star,Up08 use inventory,Down04 rotate.
Positions are signed16-bit in sixteenths of a pixel: heroX AE/B8,Y9A/A4;
starX AF/B9,Y9B/A5. HeroVX CC/VY C2;starVX CD/VY C3 are signed8-bit.
Facing030A is0left/1right;heroStatus90/starStatus91. Normal ground friction is
F689; free-flight integration F6F3. Keep source comparisons, fractions, carry,
selected terrain friction/ice and the per-frame ramp classification callbacks.
Input acceleration is +/-2 with asymmetric pre-threshold comparisons17/E9;
it can overshoot to24. Do not replace this with a symmetric constant cap.
Fresh grounded A sets VY AC(-84), followed by sourcegravity+4, first displacement
-5px. Release clamps an earlier rise below E8(-24) to E8. Actual original
30-update bounded arcs: Aheld1/4/12/32 => apex8.75/22.25/47.25/52.5px.
These execute pre-terrain input then actual original gravity/integration, with
typed contact flags. They do NOT establish landing/camera/fullMain gameplay.
This52.5px native arc needs real star-platform traversal evaluation in SMB1;
there is no approved jump-height increase or silent traversal adaptation.

Normal star charges to A0 after33 held updates (minimum valid release), A9
after42 (full charge); release at1/32 aborts. A launched star blocks new charge.

then VY+30 and facing VX table[-28,+28]. It inherits hero velocity, not a fixed
projectile vector. Wall negates savedVX; floor EORFF/CLC/ADC09 is -savedVY+8.
