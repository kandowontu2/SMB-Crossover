Additive correction: a801 first-eight rectangle derivation

The frozen mm_proto_publication_native_review_v2.py incorrectly used an
8-pixel sprite height for the derived first-eight OAM-record counts. I
withdraw those derived counts in its proof, native report and held
checkpoint. The original artifacts remain unchanged.

All 46 owned captured DMA images use 8x16 sprites: 44 have PPUCTRL $A8,
two have $AC. The separate offline correction uses each image's actual
captured PPUCTRL bit5, while rechecking all 399 complete source/physical
256-byte DMA images. It reproduces the old eight-high numbers before
replacing the derived geometry; no byte mask or native rerun is used.

Corrected actual owned-image counts (23 turns of each kind):
  Body turns with any first-eight body-record rejection: 0.
  Shot turns with any first-eight body-record rejection: 21 (formerly15).
  Body turns with any first-eight shot-record rejection: 21 (formerly16).
  Shot turns with any first-eight shot-record rejection: 0.
By the original actual visible-capture cutoffs, the longest consecutive
body-record rejection run is two frames; shot-record rejection is three.
These are visible-Y rectangle-selection counts, not opaque pixel or
per-projectile lifetime guarantees. Transparent halves of a 16-high
sprite still consume hardware selection slots.

Unchanged: all actual pixels, 46 consumed source/ack phase turns, 399
exact physical DMA images, real399vs402 cadence failure, three compiled
Vine partial-writer body-overlap counterexamples, HELD status. A geometry
correction cannot qualify or repair the candidate.
