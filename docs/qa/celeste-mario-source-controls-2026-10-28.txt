  Tier0 still uses Celeste acceleration, braking, jump and fractional motion.
  Toward wall: slow slide. Up+toward wall: stamina hold; fresh A wall jump.
  Hold consumes one decimal stamina unit/update from 299. Up wall jump
  consumes 100, clamping to zero; ordinary wall kick remains available.
