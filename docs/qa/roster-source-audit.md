# Full roster source re-audit — 2026-10-16

This is the historical findings checkpoint. See [roster corrections](roster-fixes.md) for implementation progress and rechecked interpretations, including withdrawal of the unsupported hosted-Mega-Man entry-teleport finding.

All twelve characters were reviewed again against their mapped original references. **The port still has substantial source mismatches. Passing its existing 241 regression cases does not establish reference accuracy.** This audit found 27 disagreements among 78 independently specified source properties, plus additional art, controls, damage, audio and transition issues. Several failed properties share a cause; this is not a count of 27 distinct bugs.

The numeric checks ran on both MMC3 and MMC5: **24 captures, 50,400 observed frames, 156 rule evaluations**. Fifty-one properties match within the declared quantization tolerance; 27 disagree on both mappers. Mario's deliberate native-engine behavior and Rygar's native-ROM measurements are assessed separately from the ten Flash-derived guests.

The source walk/jump/update methods were followed rather than treating every declared constant as active behavior. This matters for Samus's 180 input speed being capped to 150, Bass's movement override, Sophia's gravity-free initial jump rise and effective projectile-speed clamp, and Simon's lack of a character fall-speed cap. Ryu's gravity **does** match 1400; his incorrect terminal speed is a separate issue.

The [source contract](../reference/roster-audit-contract.json) gives every numeric expectation a source file, line, anchor and formula. [The comparison table](../../build/roster-audit/comparison-table.md) and [full measurements](../../build/roster-audit/comparisons.json) retain failures. Typical tolerances are 0.004 NES pixels/update for Q8.8 quantities, not assertions of mathematical equality. Coordinates convert by `/120` for velocities and `/7200` for accelerations at 60 Hz.

The 21 observation scenes cover walking/running, held/released jumps, falling, airborne direction release, water jumps/bottom movement, held/repeated shots in all three power states, air aiming, crouching, secondary input, Up while moving, movement during attacks, second-jump inputs and Down+B during a real jump. These are **explicit RAM fixtures**, not controller-only level clears. Direct power-state injection does not prove suit palette loading. Real pickup/lives/audio checks are separately identified in the existing suite. Logical flat-world fixtures also do not redraw all background nametables, so their screenshots are for actor inspection, not background/collision alignment claims.

Source-file hashes/method locations, compiled damage bytes, static sprite budgets, per-character contact sheets and original-game observations are under `build/roster-audit/`. Representative actor frames for every character were inspected. Full original-source equivalence is not claimed for any character.

## Highest-priority findings

1. **Empty sprite data can corrupt OAM.** `MetaspriteRenderLoop` enters its part loop when the length is zero. Luigi's crouch/hurt/death entries in all power states alias an empty metasprite. Ordinary controller-only Small Luigi Down input reproduces scattered tiles across the screen, with the allocation cursor wrapping to zero. [Screenshot](../../build/roster-audit/luigi-crouch/555.png). Small Luigi should remain standing when Down is pressed, per `MarioBase.checkState`.
2. **Luigi's large-form atlas is cropped incorrectly.** The source sheet's measured full-width grid lines are at Y `0,6,31,56,81,131,181,231,281`. Its large rows have a **50-pixel pitch**; the generator uses 41 and crops only 40 pixels. This clips large poses and selects the wrong rows. The empty crouch/death cells are an additional mapping error. [Source sheet with the current, incorrect generator grid overlaid](../../build/roster-audit/luigi-atlas-labelled.png).
3. **Sophia's missile art is empty.** Missile type 7 selects `XgSOPHIAWeapon8`, whose data length is zero, exposing the same renderer problem. A separate controller-only repro confirms that unpowered Select allocates a type-6 shot at uninitialized X/Y/Y-high zero. `FireShot` writes the active type before rejecting an unavailable missile. The source rejects unavailable special weapons without creating a projectile.
4. **Source physics are still wrong for several guests.** Pit has no jump-release friction and falls at 5 instead of 3.75 px/update. Ryu caps at 5 instead of 5.833333. Sophia applies gravity during the source's gravity-free initial rise, starts horizontal movement at full speed instead of accelerating, and caps falling at 4 instead of 4.166667. Luigi uses the same 9/256 acceleration with and without B instead of source accelerations 200/7200 and 285/7200.
5. **Several weapons use generic behavior.** Pit's arrow and Ryu's small shuriken move at 3 rather than 4.166667 and 5 px/update. Sophia's cannon auto-fires on held B and allows four bullets; the source uses fresh attack presses and a three-bullet limit. Link's yellow boomerang starts at 4.167969 instead of 2.5 and uses a generic timed return. Bill's Down+B during a jump fires horizontally instead of straight down.
6. **Simon's overload is broader than previously documented.** Twelve generated left/right attack poses exceed eight sprites per scanline. The ordinary and flame whip reach nine; the middle power state's standing strike reaches **14**. Priority rotation distributes missing pixels; it does not make those poses fit.

The empty Mega Man fire-hurt data was also found. Its runtime reachability was not established, so it is recorded as a latent asset risk rather than a reproduced Mega Man failure.

## Shared systems and intentional adaptations

**Combat and blocks.** The 41 compiled damage-table bytes were checked against the assembly; the guest-health table matches the older hosted reference for its original roster, but the 2.02 characters inherit different enemy HP than their reference. The common block dispatcher still has multi-hit bricks, reward activation, debris, coin limits and terrain-provenance protection covered by regressions. Native integer enemy hurtboxes, head probes and scripted collisions are not the source's fractional geometry. Where a source requests a white hit silhouette, storing a two-update flash timer does not render it.

**Environment.** Native SMB rooms, pipes, moving platforms, vines, flagpoles and area transitions remain the substrate. Ground/water/pipe fixture coverage exists for all characters, but that is not source identity for every transition. Only Mega Man and Samus receive explicit source spring velocities in `MegaManSpringVelocity`; the later guests retain the native fallback. Ryu/Sophia wall movement is a simplified state machine, especially top-of-wall launch, rotation and attachment transitions.

**Power-ups and palettes.** The original hosted roster's three power forms are represented; the four 2.02 additions instead compress their independent inventory/ammo upgrades into mushroom/flower tiers. Pit/Rygar also have adapted tiers. Existing real pickup tests establish 60-update pose holds, state changes, restored port palettes and correct world-object colors, not that invented tier colors equal the source. The fresh observations found **zero world-palette deviations** from red/white/tan across 50,400 rows. Directly injecting a power byte is deliberately not accepted as evidence of correct suit palette changes. Ryu/Sophia's normal inventory paths retain their default bitmap palette rather than the port's imposed three-color tier progression; legacy `REPL_COLOR` and manual-state helpers must not be mistaken for normal 2.02 upgrade behavior.

**Art and OAM.** Every generated left/right body/weapon metasprite and alias was scanned, including empty data. Peak packed rows: Mega Man 7, Samus 6, Bill 8, Link 5, Simon 14, Pit 4, Rygar 3, Luigi 4 for nonempty poses, Bass 8, Ryu 6, Sophia 5. Composite projectiles/enemies can still exceed eight. Body-budget success does not validate a crop or make an empty pose safe. The older conclusion that only Simon's flame form overloads is superseded by this scan. Ryu's ground art reused for crouch/air attacks and Sophia's missing rotated wall/flame sequences are confirmed in the generator and source animation branches.

**Audio.** Original guests use imported native drivers and explicit area/event routing; existing tests observe real APU/MMC5 output, mixing and restoration. That establishes delivery, not every source-specific waveform. Bill/Link/Simon reuse some attack/hit cues and retain simplified death behavior; source landing-event coverage is incomplete and Contra/Zelda DPCM remains disabled. The six additions use captured stage streams looping every 5,400 updates, the same theme for different room types, and shared SMB death/victory/common SFX. Source event/area music, hover/step loops and individual effect envelopes are not fully present. MMC5's extra channels increase available voices but do not supply these missing event handlers.

**Title, selection, lives and death.** Static title/no demo, a separate twelve-character select screen and highlighted-only name are explicit user requirements and intentional differences from the source UI. All twelve lives portraits and power-up transitions have current regression cases. Mega Man/Samus have their own death modules; Bill/Link/Simon have simplified timing/poses and the six additions share a 150-update death timer. This does not implement each source's particles, corpse motion, landing-dependent delays and fanfare.
