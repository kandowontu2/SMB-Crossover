Confirmed defect 1: TMNT Kiai pickup moved away from its visible sprite
--------------------------------------------------------------------
src/tmnt_equipment.s:234 TnPickupCollisionDispatch overwrote a native
screen-relative collision box with Enemy_X_Position (world low byte).
The real PowerUpObjHandler sequence first calculates RelativeEnemyPosition,
offscreen bits and GetEnemyBoundBox, then calls the private pickup gate.
The new item drawing already subtracts ScreenLeft; its collision rewrite did not.

Assembled legal full-health flower reveal, turtle0, actor5, world X968,
camera X848, visible X120: baseline wrote box [204,124,212,134], whereas its
visible icon requires [124,124,132,134]. First wrong write is bank87 PC$A920
to $0670, value204. This can miss a visible item or collide at a false location.

Private repair outlines the existing 16-byte horizontal span to an appended
same-bank helper. It uses the already-current Enemy_Rel_XPos and reapplies
the native CheckRightScreenBBox edge clipping. No new RAM or art. All 3,696
scrolled-box cases also execute the actual SMB PlayerCollisionCore at the
visible and old false locations. Another 180 cases cover partial left/right
edges. Twenty-four per-turtle/slot cases preserve the full 48-update physical
rise, reject collection during emergence, then collect the actual Kiai reward.

Confirmed defect 2: Mac extra lives erase stars, stamina and active attacks
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
src/little_mac_core.s:38 LmEnsure treated every change in NumberofLives as a
new life. A legal 1-Up changing 2 to3 replaced HP37/stars3/stamina9/attack24
with HP96/stars0/stamina20/attack0. First wrong write: bank93 PC$A03D to $5F20.
Score/coin extra lives reach the same native life counter, so share this defect.

Private repair outlines the original life test without moving its following
instructions. An appended helper compares current and prior counts, records
gains immediately, and resets boxing state on a decrease. Recording gains is
essential: a subsequent death must not compare equal to the stale old marker.
3,528 unchanged/gain/loss/gain-then-death CPU cases pass, plus area/new-game
controls. Active-play life counts 0..99 are covered; game-over underflow is not
a claimed live LmEnsure state. Native lifecycle qualification remains required.
