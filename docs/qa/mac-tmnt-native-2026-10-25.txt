Counter semantics inspected rather than assumed
----------------------------------------------
Native coin and delayed score/1-Up awards use INC NumberofLives, including
255->0. A short positive modular delta must retain boxing state at that
wrap. Death uses DEC, including0->255, then the original signed BPL rule.
The patch leaves that rule unchanged: death128->127 continues, whereas
death129->128 reaches Game Over. New-game initialization clears LmReady
through X5ProfilesNewGame before defaults are re-established. Recording
each gain matters: gain2->3 followed by death3->2 must reset correctly.
The modular helper assumes bounded native counter changes; 127+ concurrent
gains are outside qualification. No native path produces such an event.
