Sophia failure and private repair

Source Sophia.as AMMO_DEPLETION_ARR charges3 and pressSpcBtn calls the original
SophiaBullet.launchMissiles triplet. The port's SophiaInput spends first, then
attempts three separate shared-pool allocations. Cannon impact effects retain
type6 and their short positive impact timer while not counting as active cannon
shots. Homing missiles also occupy the shared pool, so a crowded pool is a legal
component condition. Native controller reachability is NOT established here.

cpu-v6/sophia-triplet-allocation.json records both facings and aim directions.
First wrong write: bank59, CPU $A2F6, InvAmmo+$B1 = $7D53,15 ->12, while only
zero/one/two slots are free. Actual created standard missiles are0/1/2.

Private proposal-v3/candidate.nes:
3e3c67648e238cd71e1c581e05773579094351f8ed1c2b7c9de59219728ad794.
24 changed bytes, bank6 only. Two JSR operand bytes retarget the existing
Sophia-only count call to a22-byte tail helper. It scans for three free slots
before the old active-standard-volley count and ammo debit. Fewer slots leave
ammo and actors untouched. Existing exported PCs, RAM addresses and all CHR
are exact. The helper occupies existing SPECIAL_EXTRA tail space; no new RAM.

proposal-v3/cpu-v2/proof.json passes the same bounded character checks.
proposal-v3/triplet-v1/proof.json additionally passes:
-All1,024 shared-pool occupancy masks: three shots/cost3, or no shots/cost0.
-160 existing-standard-missile refusals, across all ten slot positions,
 both facings, horizontal/upward aim and empty/low/full ammo.
-80 exact old/new complete-handler application-RAM and mapper comparisons
 when three slots fit, including ammo0/2/3/15. Only hardware stack, mapper
 register transactions and original SMC/call bookkeeping are excluded.

Earlier proposal-v1/v2 linker failures are preserved. They exceeded the nearly
full bank; v3's smaller helper fits without moving any pre-existing symbol.
