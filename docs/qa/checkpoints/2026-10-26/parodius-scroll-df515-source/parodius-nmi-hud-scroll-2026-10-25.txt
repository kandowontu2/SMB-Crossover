Native UpdateScreen readsPPUSTATUS then always ends with two zeroPPUSCROLL writes.
Each command applies MirrorPPUCTRL after its address header. Capture subsequently
uses the necessaryPPUADDR3F00 pair and32 ordered palette reads, overwriting t.

later applies its native PPUCTRL AND#FC HUD policy, leaving the unwanted$3300

its own UpdateScreen; without that flush the defect remains.

The successor restores the same two native zeroScroll writes at the capture-only

it does not replace the host's shadow policy with a global unconditional reset.
Capture ends with write-toggle0, so this pair addresses horizontal then vertical
scroll. The CPU model separately checks initial latch states and final fineX.
