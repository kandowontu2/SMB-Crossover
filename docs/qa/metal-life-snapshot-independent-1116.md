## Arithmetic and legitimate writer domain

CMP's N is bit7 of `(live-saved)&255`; incoming V is not part of BPL.
The detached arithmetic check covers all65,536 byte pairs and all256
equal/+1/−1 transitions. Equal and +1 store, including7F→80 andFF→00.
−1 constructs, including80→7F and00→FF. More generally deltas00..7F store;
80..FF construct. This is modulo classification, not universal signed or
unsigned ordering of arbitrary large differences.

The relevant normal source writers are native extra-life popup INC
(`FloateyNumbersCore`), hundred-coin INC (`GiveOneCoin`) and death DEC
(`PlayerLoseLife`). Award paths are small positive increments; ordinary death
is −1. Repeated accepted movement snapshots prevent accumulation across
ordinary frames. Multiple same-frame award slots are still a small positive
delta, not a128-count jump. PrimaryGameSetup's reset to2 and player/area/new
life transitions remain governed by the unchanged Fresh/ready/player guards;
they must not be reinterpreted as earned positive-life deltas. Artificial
large counter replacements and poisoned private snapshot states are outside
this legal-writer argument and are explicitly not claimed to be ordered.

## ABI, timing and remaining source gates

For equal lives, CMP already supplies the same A/X/Y/P as the old branch;
the added STA is idempotent and preserves flags. Branches stay on pageD4.
The equal path adds exactly four cycles. The new gain path intentionally
skips the constructor; a whole-old/new equivalence claim on earned inventory
would therefore be wrong.

BeforeStep first calls Ms24HurtTick. Its initial LDA GamePauseStatus / AND1
overwrites N/Z before any branch on them, and its paths provide explicit
SEC/CLC results. Public MsRRestore restores saved A/X/Y/P and returns handled
carry. No incoming-V dependency is introduced by the new branch. These are
source contracts, not a substitute for actual new public caller/status/SMC/
stack comparisons. Reached IRQ/busy-NMI cuts, stale-header/player/Fresh cases,
death/respawn and the frozen genuine1UP retained-armor/+25 replay remain the
Metal source-test owner's required evidence before physical/native review.
