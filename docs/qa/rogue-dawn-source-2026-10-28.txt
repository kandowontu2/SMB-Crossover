Actual bank and owner map
-------------------------
Notation physical bank DECIMAL / CPU address; machine-readable map additionally
uses HEX physical bank prefixes (3D=61,3E=62,3F=63). File offset for these8KiB
banks is16+8192*bank+(CPU&1FFF). Fixed C/E engines are62:C000 and63:E000.
Vectors are NMI62:CAB7, reset63:FFB0, IRQ62:CAE9.
62:C716 maps physical61 at A000 via63:FFDC. FFDC writes MMC3 registers plus
foreground shadows7820/7821/7822+register. 62:CA46 restores area mapping.
62:C4FA maps an area's logical16KiB pair at8000/A000. Never call an8000/A000
address without its actual physical bank. In particular61:A000 is MAP UI.

Player update62:CC0D; action tableCC22:0standCC36,1run/run-jumpDA25->CCC2,
2jumpDA0A->D002,3ballD0E1,4aimD198,5doorD3A8,6jump-aimDA72,
7deadD41A,8dead2D41F,9elevatorD423. These distinct handlers matter.

Morph is unconditional at62:D0B5, Long checks replaced with constant04 in
native fire/update code. Wall Jump requires gear04, freshA(12 bit80), held
horizontal(14 bits1/2), then blocked-side probe carryclear before nativeCD40.
Its physical probe-side/facing sequence remains a runtime observation need.

Spring uses freshA plus actual frozen support7D or down probeE7AD. Its native
release edgeCD/14 immediately clips upward VY less thanFE toFE. Ordinary
standing-jump D002 cuts only after jump displacement030F>=32. Do not apply
one jump-release policy to every source action. Air morph uses freshDown and
Spring bit10/support with a2A cooldown on action2. Root must retain these gates.

REAL ball landing bounce is63:E2E8..E318 after actual downward collision:
action3 halves VY/fraction and negates the pair; tiny impacts stop. The separate
62:D9D4 extension belongs to the unmorph/support path, not the whole landing
bounce. Normal ground contact stops vertical state/clears gravity.

Bomb placement uses B(bit40 of12|16), notA. It now permits airborne placement,
still rejects elevator0307, and shares exactly3 slots D0/E0/F0 with beams and
missiles. Source actions8->9->10. Fuse starts12 and decrements only when
FrameCount2D bit0=1:12 eligible ticks, normally24 Main updates. Original NES
Metroid fuse was24. Full explosion block/contact/animation lifecycle remains
unported; do not substitute a generic host bomb or assume global enemy damage.

Wave40 and Ice80 coexist, in either acquisition order. Ice changes action3
but preserves Wave metadata; DA85 selects native Wave motion when40. Actual
Wave tables are62:D58B..D5C0. The signed motion decoder bytes at CPU8296/832F
match exactly in physical banks2/4/6/8/10; they do NOT match title bank0.
Missiles debit6879 once, clear mode010E atzero, and removed D0-only restriction
allows other free shared projectile slots. Enemy-type damage handlingDA92 and
real contactF2CA must remain owned, not guessed from beam name.
