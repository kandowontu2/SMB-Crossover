## Material next-plan requirements

The concrete new target is a checked direct CF ingress plus removal of
unnecessary generic caller transports, rather than a raw tile memo or a
small division-only trial. Mini's transport illustrates the correct shape:
full authentication first, direct resident vector, explicit C-window/P/A
restoration and unchanged failure fallback. CF additionally needs its actual
D0→C2→D0 handling and CfArgA/P/target fields preserved; the rejected C2-only
generation did not meet that domain. Reusing real CfCallA is safer than
silently omitting those fields, but the old estimated6550-cycle concept alone
still leaves normal Main around49.7k before NMI.

A separate native-only geometry contract could target slots10/12/13/14:
seven invocations currently cost12163 query cycles for about676 actual leaf
cycles, a11487-cycle transport ceiling before replacement overhead. The
adder/box leaves are real Y0/Y12 getters; head/side use native position,
crouch, seven offset entries and native block RAM, but also write existing
CFProbeIndex/Mode in C2EA/EB. Promoting them to immutable native code would
require complete consumer/liveness proof of those private scratch writes,
whole public return/CVID/ZP/SMC/stack parity and a separately reviewed explicit
invalid-cache admission contract. Public CfCheck must stay full and private
services must remain authenticated. The visible predicate is left separate:
its conditional admission must not be widened just to recover another1618.
No opcode or proposed array for any such native contract has been written.

That geometry ceiling plus the separate historical marker3966 still leaves
about40.8k Main before native replacement overhead and regular NMI. Additional
generic-caller/direct-transport savings are required; this is a potentially
material avenue to measure later, not a60Hz claim or a complete funded plan.
Even the older impossible zero-routing floor plus normal NMI was about33.7k.
Any promised one-frame result needs further real outside-caller reductions
and a realistic per-call transport allowance, not zero-cost routing.

Current source-derived capacity leads remain scoped: private A2 tail79 is
available only inside its owned loaded image, and native CF guard tail14 is
explicit linker capacity. Private-tail code cannot be executed before its
full validation and is not immutable native-helper storage. The shared native

paths; their sizes are not free padding. The old caller-plane55→30 theoretical
deduplication is at most25 bytes and still lacks complete external-reader and
containing-extent closure. No current capacity supports the larger plan yet.
These ingress/liveness/capacity questions need a detached descriptor/root
review before any source overlay; the old587/b8bd grants are not widened.
