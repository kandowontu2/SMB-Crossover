# Credits

## SMB Crossover for NES

- **Kandowontu** leads the NES adaptation, including cartridge integration, character programming, and release maintenance. [Project repository](https://github.com/kandowontu2/SMB-Crossover)
- **Jay Pavlina and the Exploding Rabbit team** created the original *Super Mario Bros. Crossover* browser game. Its in-game credits name **Jay Pavlina** for design and programming, **Zach Robinson** for custom graphics, **Mathew Valente** for custom music, and **sbq92** for additional skins. Its character behavior, NES sprite atlases, equipment systems, and music selections informed this adaptation. [Original source](https://github.com/JayPavlina/super-mario-bros-crossover) · [Playable browser reference](https://www.smbgames.be/smb-crossover.php) · [Version and extraction notes](docs/reference/expanded.md)
- The original browser game also credits **TBA**, **aliceandsven**, **ArtSorr0w**, **Ganymede**, and **Kyoo** for testing. It thanks **Iggy**, **Dwedit**, **J Squared**, the **NESdev forums**, and the **Exploding Rabbit community**. **Blargg’s Game Music Emu** powered the original browser game’s music playback. [Original source](https://github.com/JayPavlina/super-mario-bros-crossover) · [Game Music Emu](https://github.com/libgme/game-music-emu)
- **jroweboy**, **smbhacks**, **AleFunky**, **Cantersoft**, and the **SMB1Base contributors** built the ROM-hacking foundation used here. **doppelganger** authored the *Super Mario Bros.* disassembly credited in the source. [SMB1Base](https://github.com/smbstudio/smb1base) · [Upstream contributors](https://github.com/smbstudio/smb1base/graphs/contributors) · [Disassembly](https://www.romhacking.net/documents/344/) · [Original source credit](inc/common.inc)
- **Nintendo and SRD** made the original *Super Mario Bros.* **Shigeru Miyamoto** and **Takashi Tezuka** designed the game, **Toshihiko Nakago and SRD** programmed it, and **Koji Kondo** composed the music. [Nintendo’s interview with the original team](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wii/mario25th/4/0/)
